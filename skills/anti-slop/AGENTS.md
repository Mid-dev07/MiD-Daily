# Anti-AI-Slop compact spine

This file is vendored from `dharmawan-id/anti-ai-slop` as the runnable local reference for MiD Daily. It is an editorial style standard, not an authorship detector.

## Core rules

- No em dash in published artifacts. Avoid double-hyphen in published artifacts.
- Avoid current model-heavy vocabulary such as "menyoroti", "menggarisbawahi", "memfasilitasi", "mengoptimalkan", "komprehensif", "holistik", "krusial", "transformasi digital", and similar filler when a plain concrete word works.
- Cut stacked transition fillers such as "Selain itu", "Di sisi lain", "Lebih lanjut", and "Dengan demikian" when they add no meaning.
- Avoid triple-adjective strings, forced parallelism, repeated question restatement, and short-section summary padding.
- Avoid contrast framing such as "bukan X, tetapi Y" when the affirmative claim can be stated directly.
- Avoid false agency such as saying data, reports, frameworks, or interfaces "understand", "decide", or "tell us".
- Remove trailing participle padding such as "menyoroti...", "menggarisbawahi...", or "memastikan..." when it adds no new fact.
- Cut adverbs that do not change the claim.
- Vary sentence and paragraph length. Avoid repeated three-sentence blocks.
- Keep entity names stable instead of cycling synonyms.
- Name a source when making an attribution.
- Reserve hedging for real uncertainty.
- Avoid rhetorical questions, mic-drop closers, performed authenticity, sycophantic openers, and generic turn-closers.
- Watch 2026 model tells: colon-heavy elaboration, semicolon spikes, affirmative pivots, staccato triplets, fixed "significance" collocations, and overly dense heading/bold formatting.

## Indonesian-specific rules

- Prefer "yang" over calque-like "yang mana" or "di mana" relative connectors.
- Avoid bureaucratic connector clusters such as "terlepas dari hal tersebut", "kendati demikian", and "berkenaan dengan".
- Avoid definition-first openings when a direct statement works.
- Avoid inflated uses of "menjadi".
- Avoid formulaic calls to action such as "Yuk simak!", "Tunggu apa lagi?", and "Jangan sampai ketinggalan!".
- Natural particles such as "nah", "sih", "dong", and "kan" are allowed when they fit the user's actual voice; do not add them mechanically.

Detect -> rewrite -> reread -> rewrite again when needed.
