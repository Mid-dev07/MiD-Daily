# MiD Daily Design Direction

## Identity
MiD is a calm, premium personal operating workspace. The Unreal layer elevates material, depth, lighting, interaction, and atmosphere without turning MiD into a game menu, cyberpunk dashboard, or generic glassmorphism product.

## Experience priorities
1. Experience
2. Clarity
3. Performance
4. Aesthetic

## Character
- daylight-first and nature-informed
- calm, mineral, engineered
- original rather than derivative
- realistic unreal nature: believable material and environmental response without imitation of a specific game
- information remains primary; atmosphere remains contextual

## Palette
- base: #0a1119
- deep surface: #0d151f
- mineral surfaces: #0f1a26, #142130, #1b2b3d, #24384e
- signature accent: #4fd1ff
- semantic success: #4ade80
- semantic warning: #fbbf24
- semantic danger: #fb7185
- natural secondary tones are restrained and subordinate to the neutral material system

## Light and material
- canonical key light: 145deg
- material roles: solid/mineral, glass shell, recessed, emissive
- glass is an accent material, not a universal treatment
- emissive is reserved for meaningful focus or status
- recessed controls should feel inset rather than floating
- depth comes from hierarchy, contact, edge response, and light, not from adding effects everywhere

## Typography
- UI/display: Inter
- mono only for indices, timestamps, telemetry, and compact metadata
- typography must prioritize readability over technical styling

## Spatial language
- numbered workspace system
- editorial hierarchy
- generous negative space where it improves focus
- living environment provides context, not decoration
- DOM remains authoritative for product information
- WebGL is bounded to environmental context only

## Motion
- short, purposeful response
- transform/opacity/local light catch preferred
- no perpetual decorative loops
- reduced-motion mode must preserve hierarchy without non-essential movement

## Responsive direction
- phone, tablet, laptop, and desktop are intentional layout states
- mobile is a reflowed composition, not a squeezed desktop
- preserve touch targets and content readability

## Liveliness
MiD should feel alive through real context, state, hierarchy, environment, and interaction. It must not rely on generic gradients, glow, decorative badges, fake activity, fake metrics, or animation wallpaper.
