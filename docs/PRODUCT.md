# MiD-Daily — Product Baseline

## Vision
MiD-Daily is a lightweight daily management application combining scheduling, tasks, finance tracking, and a dashboard.

## Initial target
Personal use first, with architecture suitable for small-scale multi-user expansion.

## Core priorities
1. Functional
2. Stable
3. Secure
4. Deployable
5. Maintainable
6. Extensible

## Core modules

### Schedule / Agenda
Flexible entries for class, work, meeting, study, personal activity, appointment, event, and other activities.

### Tasks
CRUD, status, priority, deadline, category, and progress.

### Finance
Income and expense records with category, date, amount, notes, and summaries.

### Dashboard
Today's schedule, upcoming tasks, progress, finance summary, and important reminders.

## Future
Authentication, Telegram, WhatsApp, social analytics, and AI assistance.
